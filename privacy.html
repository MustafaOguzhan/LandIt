2. What we collect

Account information

  • Email address and password (your password is never stored in readable form — Supabase Auth, our authentication provider, stores only a salted hash).
  • The date your free trial started, and your subscription status, so we know whether to grant access to paid features.

Resume and interview content

  • Whatever you type into the resume builder — name, target role, contact details, an optional photo, work history, education, and skills — if you're logged in, this is saved to your account so it's there next time you visit.
  • Job descriptions you paste into the Keyword Targeting tool, and your answers during an AI mock interview session.

Payment information

We never see or store your card number. Checkout and billing are handled entirely by Stripe; we only receive your subscription status (active, past due, canceled) and a Stripe customer reference back from them.

Technical information

Standard web request data (IP address, browser user-agent) is processed transiently by our hosting provider and, for job searches in certain countries, passed to that search provider as required by their API — see section 3.

4. Why we're allowed to process this (legal basis)

  • Contract: account data, resume data, and payment data — we need this to actually provide the Service you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interest: the churn-feedback emails, and basic technical logs used to keep the Service running securely.
  • Consent: anything beyond the above, if we ever ask for it separately.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account and resume data for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account (Account panel → Delete account) removes your profile and resume data immediately and permanently, and cancels any active subscription — this is a real, self-serve action, not a request you have to email in for.

If you never create an account, nothing you type into the resume builder is stored anywhere — it only exists in your browser tab.

6. Your rights

If you're in the EEA/UK (which includes Norway), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Delete your data (or just do it yourself from the Account panel)
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority — in Norway, that's Datatilsynet

To exercise any of these, email [privacy contact email].

7. Cookies and local storage

LandIt doesn't use advertising or analytics cookies. Our authentication provider (Supabase) stores your login session in your browser's local storage so you stay logged in between visits — this is functionally necessary for the Service to work and isn't used for tracking.

8. International data transfers

Some of our processors (listed in section 3) may store or process data outside the EEA, including in the United States. Where that happens, we rely on their standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards as required under GDPR.

9. Children

LandIt isn't intended for anyone under 16. We don't knowingly collect data from children under that age.

10. Security

Your resume and account data is protected by database-level access rules (Row Level Security) so that only you can ever read or write your own data — this is enforced by the database itself, not just application code. All traffic to and from LandIt is encrypted (HTTPS).

11. Changes to this policy

If we make a material change to how we handle your data, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, notify you by email.
